Mobile app / Dating experience

Vyybee

A Gen Z dating app designed around compatibility, post-chat chemistry, and playful vibe scoring instead of another endless swipe loop.

Role UI/UX Design, product flow, visual direction
Tools Figma, prototyping, wireframing
Audience Gen Z users looking for expressive dating experiences

Design context

Dating apps often stop at the match. Vyybee extends the experience after two people start talking, using compatibility checks, a journey view, and a visible vibe score to help users understand whether the conversation has real chemistry.

The screens from Figma show a distinctive pink-led design system, rounded mobile cards, emotional labels, and playful micro-moments like “Ideal Match,” “Chemistry,” “Comfort,” and “Discovery.”

Design approach

Make matching feel more personal and less mechanical.

Compatibility checks

The app turns matching into a lightweight reflection moment where users compare interests, intentions, and relationship signals.

Vibe score feedback

The score visual makes abstract chemistry easier to understand and gives the product a memorable signature interaction.

Post-chat journey

Instead of ending at chat, Vyybee shows progress through view score, chemistry, chat, comfort, like, compatibility, and discovery.

Outcome

A dating product with a distinct emotional tone.

The case study now presents Vyybee through real Figma screens, with the strongest emphasis on its differentiator: helping users evaluate chemistry after a chat through a guided, visual, emotionally clear journey.
