Mobile app / Dating experience

Vyybee

A Gen Z dating app concept designed around personality, expression, and low-pressure discovery instead of swipe fatigue.

Role UI/UX Design, product flow, visual direction
Tools Figma, prototyping, wireframing
Audience Gen Z users looking for expressive dating experiences

Design context

Dating apps often feel repetitive: profile, swipe, match, chat. Vyybee reframes the interaction around vibe, interests, and quick expression so users can understand personality before starting a conversation.

The design direction uses bold gradients, high-contrast surfaces, playful profile moments, and simple actions to make discovery feel energetic without becoming overwhelming.

Design approach

Make matching feel more personal and less mechanical.

Vibe-first profiles

Profiles prioritize mood, interests, and visual expression so users can quickly decide whether someone feels aligned.

Clear match moments

Match states are designed as celebratory transitions that encourage the next action without forcing it.

Lightweight chat

Conversation screens keep messages clean and approachable, helping new matches start with less friction.
